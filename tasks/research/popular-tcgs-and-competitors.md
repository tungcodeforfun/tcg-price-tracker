# Popular TCGs and competitor scan (research, 2026-09-25)

Goal: pick the games to launch with, and map the paid collection and price-tracker market for a freemium TCG tracker.
Rules: primary sources wherever possible. Each claim has a URL. Anything I couldn't confirm from a primary source is marked **[UNVERIFIED]**. Anything I reasoned out myself is marked **[INFERENCE]**.

---

## TL;DR

**Launch games (5):** Pokémon (English and Japanese), Magic: The Gathering, One Piece Card Game, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Riftbound. **Fast-follow:** Disney Lorcana. **Watchlist:** Gundam, Dragon Ball Super Fusion World, Star Wars: Unlimited, Digimon.

If you can only launch **3**, pick Pokémon, One Piece and Magic.

**Competitor pricing:** consumer tiers cluster between **$2.49 and $9.99 a month**. Tiers above that ($24.99 to $49 a month) are sold as seller or retailer tools. The main free-app threat is Collectr: it has a strong free tier, 25+ games, and PRO at $59.99 a year. The TCGplayer app is 100% free.

**Gaps a new product could win on:**
1. Showing which source each price comes from, and blending several sources.
2. Real P&L: realized gains and a record of sold cards.
3. Price alerts on owned cards and wishlists, included in a cheap tier.
4. First-class Japanese Pokémon plus sealed and graded products, in one portfolio.
5. Supporting new games quickly (e.g. Riftbound).
6. Data export and a web app in the free tier, instead of behind a paywall.

### 2.2 Patterns

1. **Scanning is table stakes and usually free, or free with a cap.**
   - Free or unlimited: TCGplayer, Ludex, ManaBox.
   - Collectr and Deckbox gate *unlimited* scanning.
   - A paywall on scanning alone is weak. **[INFERENCE]**

2. **Paywalls typically cover the following.** Exceptions: Collectr's price-history depth isn't gated, and PriceCharting's value chart is free.
   - Deep price history (Collectr: 5+ years).
   - Export (Collectr, TCG Collector; PriceCharting limits it).
   - Collection-size or list caps (CardCastle 1,000 cards; Ludex 60; TCG Collector 300 per list; CollX).
   - Alerts (PriceCharting Collector).
   - P&L (PriceCharting profit/cost chart).

3. **Price bands:**
   - Hobbyist: **$2.49–$9.99/mo**, or **$23–$100/yr**.
   - Power, seller or retailer: **$24.99/mo** (Ludex Pro) to **$49/mo** (PriceCharting Legendary, which includes API and CSV).

4. **Most collector apps track a portfolio's value but are thin on real P&L.**
   - Collectr shows cost basis and gains/losses.
   - PriceCharting charges for a profit/cost chart.
   - Legacy Dragon Shield tracks sold-card value "won or lost": https://about.dragonshield.com/gaming-inspiration/new-features-now-available-on-card-manager/
   - None of the official pages I reviewed advertise realized-gain or tax-lot reporting. **[INFERENCE from absence]**

5. **Where prices come from is often opaque or single-source.**
   - The TCGplayer app shows TCGplayer Market Price.
   - Collectr says "real sales data" without naming sources on the pages I reviewed.
   - Dragon Shield (MTG) and ManaBox show several vendors side by side.
   - PriceCharting is based on eBay sales.
   - JustTCG markets in-store sales blended with online data as a differentiator: https://justtcg.com/

6. **Game coverage is split into two camps.**
   - Generalists: Collectr (25+ games), TCGplayer (dozens).
   - Specialists: MTG-only (ManaBox, Deckbox, CardCastle); Pokémon-only (TCG Collector); six games (Sorted).
   - New games reach the specialists late. As fetched, Sorted doesn't list Riftbound, Gundam or DBS FW, and the TCGplayer app listing text omits Riftbound.

## 3. Gaps a new product could win on

Ranked by expected leverage for a paid freemium SaaS. **[INFERENCE, grounded in §1–2]**

1. **Transparent, multi-source "fair value".**
   - Show each price's source (TCGplayer market, eBay sold, Cardmarket, in-store via JustTCG), with a confidence or spread band and a last-sold timestamp.
   - Competitors show either one number with no stated source, or a vendor list with no synthesis.
   - This matters most for volatile, speculation-driven lines (Lorcana, DBS FW, Riftbound, per TCGplayer's own commentary).

2. **Real portfolio accounting as the paid core.**
   - Cost basis by lot, realized versus unrealized P&L, and a sold-card ledger.
   - Sealed products valued against MSRP.
   - CSV export that tax software can read.
   - Today this is either missing or scattered across separate paywalls (PriceCharting $6 for the profit chart, Collectr gains without a realized ledger).

3. **Alerts in a cheap tier.**
   - Price moves on owned cards, wishlist price-below-market alerts, and sealed-over-MSRP alerts.
   - PriceCharting charges for these. Sorted lists them as "coming soon". Collectr's PRO page doesn't list them.
   - This is a natural reason to pay for games whose value follows the meta (One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Riftbound).

4. **Japanese Pokémon as a first-class citizen, with sealed and graded products, in one multi-game portfolio.**
   - Pokémon Japan is a top-10 TCGplayer line on its own.
   - TCG Collector nails the JP variant database but is Pokémon-only and weak on pricing.
   - Collectr is multi-game but isn't JP-focused.

5. **Supporting new games quickly.**
   - Riftbound (#5–#6 by GMV) and Gundam arrived within the last year.
   - Specialist apps lag, and a provider already covers both (JustTCG).
   - Promise and measure a "set added within N days of release" SLA.

6. **Trust and data ownership.**
   - Free CSV export, a web app on par with mobile, and no lock-in.
   - Several competitors paywall export (Collectr, TCG Collector) or are mobile-only with app-store-only billing (ManaBox).

7. **Don't compete on:**
   - Free scanning speed against TCGplayer.
   - MTG deckbuilding against ManaBox, Deckbox or Moxfield.
   - Marketplace or social features against Collectr, TCGplayer or CollX.
   - Position as the **portfolio and P&L layer across games**.

### Suggested price anchoring **[INFERENCE]**

- **Free:** multi-game collection, current value, basic chart, scanning (if built), export.
- **Paid, around $4.99–$6.99/mo or $49–$59/yr:** matches Collectr PRO and PriceCharting Collector. Includes:
  - multi-source fair value and history depth
  - alerts
  - P&L and sold ledger
  - unlimited portfolios, lots and graded items
- **Optional power/seller tier, around $19–$29/mo:** matches Ludex Pro. Only if bulk CSV, reprice lists or listing tools are built.
